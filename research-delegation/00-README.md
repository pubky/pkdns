# Delegation research

This research explores delegation in Pubky: giving other keys or service
providers permission to manage hosting and secure connections on a user's
behalf, while the user retains control of their identity.

Read in filename order:

1. [First principles](./01-first-principles.md): what must be preserved.
2. [Design principles](./02-design-principles.md): compatibility goals.
3. [Problem statement](./03-problem-statement.md): everyday user and operator
   needs.
4. [Terminology](./04-terminology.md): the terms used in this research.
5. [Design discussion](./05-routing-and-tls-delegation.md): a proposed
   approach and its tradeoffs.
6. [Packet size](./06-pkarr-packet-size.md): record size limits.
7. [Other projects](./07-sovereign-web-projects.md): relevant approaches and
   lessons for Pubky.
8. [DNS compatibility idea](./08-dane-idea.md): an alternative to explore.
9. [Alternative delegation model](./09-discussion.md): another approach to
   explore.
